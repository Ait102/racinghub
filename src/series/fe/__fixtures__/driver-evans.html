Mitchell William Evans (born 24 June 1994) is a New Zealand racing driver who is set to compete in Formula E for the Opel GSE Formula E Team. He previously raced for Jaguar Racing between 2016 and 2026. As of September 2026, Evans holds the record for the most total wins in Formula E, with 16.
